About this website

Disclaimer

FeelPrepared brings together information about anaesthesia and pain relief to help people feel prepared for their care. It was created by Dr Katie Turnbull, an anaesthetist in NHS Lothian, to improve the quality of care for women, children and young people.

This website is not for urgent problems. If you are worried about yourself or your child, contact the team looking after you, your GP, or NHS 24 on 111. In an emergency, call 999.

General information only

The information on this website is general guidance. It does not replace individual advice from your midwife, anaesthetist, doctor or other healthcare professional, who will take your own health and circumstances into account.

Please discuss any decisions about your care with your healthcare team. If anything here seems different from what you have been told, follow the advice of the team looking after you.

What is on this website

FeelPrepared is made up of separate sections, each with its own purpose:

  • Pain relief in labour and caesarean birth: information, leaflets and videos about pain relief options, in several languages. This section has its own disclaimer explaining how its translations were made.
  • Paediatric pain management: information for children and young people with persistent pain, their families and referring clinicians, from the pain team at the Royal Hospital for Children and Young People. This section has its own disclaimer.
  • Obstetric Anaesthetic Clinic leaflets: a service that clinic staff use to share selected leaflets with patients by QR code or link.

New sections may be added over time. Where a section needs extra information, it will have its own notice.

Keeping information up to date

We try to keep the information accurate and current. Leaflets have their own review dates, and guidance can change. If you are unsure whether something still applies to you, please ask your healthcare team.

This website links to other websites, such as NHS and OAA pages. We are not responsible for the content of other websites, and they have their own privacy policies.

Your privacy

This website does not ask for your name or any personal or medical details. Leaflet links shared by the clinic do not contain any information about you.

Some settings, such as text size, contrast and dark mode, are saved only in your own browser so they are remembered next time. Some pages count visits anonymously, without cookies and without identifying you.
